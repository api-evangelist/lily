Autonomous underwater drones are transforming coral reef conservation by delivering high resolution mapping, faster environmental assessments, and continuous ecosystem monitoring. Coral reefs support an extraordinary diversity of marine life while protecting coastlines, sustaining fisheries, and contributing billions of dollars to the global economy through tourism and ecosystem services. Despite their importance, these fragile ecosystems are under increasing pressure from climate change, ocean acidification, pollution, destructive fishing practices, and coastal development.
