How Unauthorized Drone Operations Are Becoming a Criminal Justice Concern The rapid expansion of drone technology has transformed the way people capture images, inspect infrastructure, deliver goods, and conduct commercial operations. But as unmanned aircraft become more accessible, authorities around the world are also confronting a growing category of cases involving illegal or dangerous drone activities. What was once largely viewed as a regulatory issue can, under certain circumstances, become a criminal matter.
