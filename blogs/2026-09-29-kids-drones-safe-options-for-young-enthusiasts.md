Small, beginner-friendly drones can give children an exciting introduction to flight, technology and photography. But for parents, the right first drone is less about impressive specifications and more about choosing an aircraft that matches a child's age, experience and flying environment. Drones have moved well beyond the world of professional photographers and aviation enthusiasts.
