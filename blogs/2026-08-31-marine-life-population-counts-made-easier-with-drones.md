Counting marine animals has long been one of the most challenging tasks in ocean research. Vast waters, unpredictable conditions, and the difficulty of observing animals beneath the surface have made population estimates a complex and time-consuming process. Now, drone technology is helping scientists collect more accurate data while reducing the risks and costs associated with traditional survey methods.
