Advanced aerial technology is giving scientists a clearer view of environmental changes, helping researchers monitor ecosystems with greater speed, precision, and efficiency. Climate change research has entered a new era with the widespread adoption of drone technology. Equipped with high resolution cameras, thermal sensors, multispectral imaging systems, and LiDAR equipment, drones are transforming how scientists observe environmental changes across forests, coastlines, glaciers, wetlands, and agricultural landscapes.
