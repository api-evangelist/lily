How unmanned aircraft developed from simple surveillance tools into sophisticated systems capable of shaping modern warfare Military drones have undergone a remarkable transformation over the past several decades. What began largely as a way to observe enemy positions without putting pilots at risk has evolved into a complex field involving surveillance, precision strikes, electronic warfare, communications, and autonomous capabilities. The evolution has not been driven by a single breakthrough.
