Drones have become increasingly common across the United States, from recreational aircraft flown by hobbyists to commercial systems used for photography, inspections, surveying, deliveries, and other services. As their use expands, so does the possibility of accidents. A drone can crash through a window, damage a roof, destroy landscaping, strike a vehicle, or cause other forms of property damage.
