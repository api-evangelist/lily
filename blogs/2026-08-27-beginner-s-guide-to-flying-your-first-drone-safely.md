Flying a drone for the first time can be exciting, but the experience comes with responsibilities that every beginner should understand. From choosing the right location to learning how the controls respond, safe drone operation starts well before the aircraft leaves the ground. For new pilots, the goal is simple: learn the basics, understand the risks, and build confidence gradually .
