Cities around the world are investing in air quality monitoring networks designed to provide real time insights into pollution levels and environmental conditions. These systems are becoming an essential tool for governments, researchers, and communities seeking to reduce exposure to harmful pollutants and improve urban health. Traditional air monitoring stations have often been limited in number, making it difficult to capture differences in air quality between neighborhoods.
