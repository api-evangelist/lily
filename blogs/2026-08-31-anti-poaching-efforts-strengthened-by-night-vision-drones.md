Across some of the world's most vulnerable ecosystems, conservation teams are turning to advanced technology to combat illegal wildlife trafficking. Night-vision drones are becoming a powerful tool in anti-poaching operations, helping rangers monitor protected areas, detect suspicious activity, and respond faster to threats against endangered species. Poachers often operate under the cover of darkness, taking advantage of remote landscapes and limited human visibility.
