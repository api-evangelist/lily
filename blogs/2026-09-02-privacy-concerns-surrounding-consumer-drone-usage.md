Consumer drones have moved from niche technology to everyday products. Once associated primarily with professional photography, filmmaking, and specialized surveying, drones are now increasingly accessible to ordinary consumers. Their falling prices, improved cameras, automated flight features, and compact designs have made aerial technology easier than ever to own and operate.
