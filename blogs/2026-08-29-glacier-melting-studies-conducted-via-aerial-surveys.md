Scientists are using advanced aerial surveys to monitor glacier loss, revealing critical insights into climate change and its impact on the planet’s frozen regions. A New Perspective From Above Across the world’s mountain ranges and polar landscapes, glaciers are changing at an unprecedented pace. To better understand these transformations, researchers are increasingly turning to aerial surveys, using aircraft, drones, and advanced imaging technologies to measure ice loss with remarkable precision.
