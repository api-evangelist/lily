Drones have transformed from specialized military tools into widely accessible technologies used in photography, agriculture, logistics, emergency response, infrastructure inspection, and entertainment. As drone adoption accelerates worldwide, governments face the challenge of creating regulations that balance innovation, public safety, privacy, and national security. However, there is no universal framework for drone operations.
